What is Summarization?

Summarization is the process of condensing a text to its essential points while retaining its meaning. There are two main types:

Abstractive Summarization

Abstractive summarization generates a summary that may contain new phrases and sentences not present in the original text. It interprets and paraphrases the content.

Extractive Summarization

Extractive summarization selects important sentences or phrases directly from the original text, presenting them as the summary without changes.

Why Hybrid Text Summarization?

Hybrid text summarization combines the strengths of both **Abstractive** and **Extractive Summarization** techniques. It uses the extractive approach to identify important sentences or phrases and the abstractive approach to rephrase and rewrite content for improved coherence and conciseness.

This hybrid approach ensures a summary that is both accurate (by extracting essential points) and natural (by paraphrasing for fluency).
